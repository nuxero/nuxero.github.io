I’m Hector Zelaya, a Computer Systems Engineer from El Salvador — a small and beautiful country in Central America.

What I Do Link to heading

I’m a WebRTC Developer Advocate at WebRTC.ventures, where I work on real-time communication applications, technical content, and community building. I help teams understand and adopt WebRTC, AI and DevOps.

I’m also an AWS Community Builder and a 2x AWS Certified professional. I speak at events like AWS Community Day México and write about cloud infrastructure, AI agents, and developer tooling.

In 2025 I founded the AI Tinkerers San Salvador chapter to bring together local professionals interested in artificial intelligence.
